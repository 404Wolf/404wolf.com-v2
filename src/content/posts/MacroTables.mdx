## Summary

I tend not to use tables. I use nested lists.

At Macro, I'm bringing tables backk.

Enter: the Macro table.

<BleedingVideo src="https://static-file-service.macro.com/file/e1914a10-5bf1-4a12-9ff6-17d53d936cd9" />

Bring your nested lists to Macro tables.

<BleedingVideo src="https://static-file-service.macro.com/file/a499f8e8-09ab-45f0-b777-cbf750d933b4" />

Macro tables are good. They have all the batteries 🔋:

1. Create one inline, mid-sentence, with `/`.
2. Can easily add rows or columns anywhere without spending 5 minutes
   fumbling through menus.
3. Easy to move around cells. Easy to find and use the handle to move around
   cells. Handle is not 4 pixels tall.
4. Easy to copy and delete and cut and paste. Pasting when there isn't enough
   space in the table crops what you are pasting. I feel like poofing in new
   cells is not intuitive, it's jarring.
5. Can delete a column. Can delete a row. Do not need to use nav menus to do
   so.
6. Support for lists, headings, and other nodes inside table cells.
7. Can delete the table without having to select the whole thing via obvious
   button.
8. Tables work in messages. In emails. In documents. Are consistent. (soon!)
9. Works on mobile. Can easily scroll and see the whole thing on mobile.
